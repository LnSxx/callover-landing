# Delete your Callover account

You can permanently delete your Callover account and the data associated with it.

Account deletion is irreversible. Once your account has been deleted, you will no longer be able to sign in or restore your account data.

## Delete your account in the Callover Mobile app

1. Open Callover.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Select **Delete account**.
4. Confirm the deletion by clicking **Delete account** button.

## Delete your account on the web

Use the button below to sign in and permanently delete your Callover account.

[Sign in to delete your account](/delete-account/flow)

Signing in does not delete your account automatically. You will see a separate confirmation screen before deletion.

## Data that will be deleted

When you delete your account, Callover deletes data associated with your account, including:

- Your account and profile information
- Your active sessions
- Your contacts
- Your call history
- Your notifications
- Your push notification tokens
- Your account preferences stored on our servers

Data stored locally on your devices may remain until you uninstall Callover or clear the application data.

Other users may still see information that belongs to them, such as their own call history or contact records involving your former account. Your deleted account will no longer remain active or accessible.

## Data retention

Some limited information may be retained when required by law, necessary to prevent fraud or abuse, or needed to protect the security of Callover.

Any retained information will only be used for the reason it was retained and will be deleted when it is no longer required.

## Processing time

Account deletion initiated through the application or website is normally processed immediately.

Some related data may take additional time to disappear from backups and technical systems.

## Cannot access your account?

For security reasons, we cannot delete an account based only on a username.

You must sign in to prove that you own the account. If you cannot sign in, contact Callover support to recover access before requesting deletion.

Email: [callovercommunications@gmail.com](mailto:callovercommunications@gmail.com)
