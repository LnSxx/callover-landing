# Want to Callover?

Callover is a cross-platform app for private audio and video calls.

It uses WebRTC to connect people directly whenever possible. No call recording. No transcription. No advertising profiles. No hidden analytics.

Just calls.

[Get Callover](#download) · [View source code](https://github.com/LnSxx/callover-server)

## Private by design

Your conversations are not content for us to analyse.

Callover does not use:

- Advertising trackers
- Behavioural analytics
- Conversation transcription
- Call recording
- Content recommendations
- Engagement algorithms

We collect only the data required to create your account, connect calls and keep the service working.

## Peer-to-peer communication

Audio and video are transmitted using WebRTC.

Whenever the network allows it, media travels directly between call participants. Callover servers coordinate the connection, but they do not process or store the contents of your calls.

STUNning connections. No unnecessary spectators.

## Built for calling

Callover focuses on the features needed for direct communication:

- Audio and video calls
- Contacts and favourites
- Call history
- Incoming call notifications
- Presence information
- Cross-platform communication

No feeds. No public follower counts. No engagement tricks.

This is SDP-arta.

## Your calls belong to you

Callover does not store the audio or video from your calls.

The service uses signaling infrastructure to help devices establish a connection. In some network conditions, encrypted media may pass through a TURN relay server so the call can work. The relay forwards the traffic and does not record the conversation.

Your account data can be deleted from the application or through the account deletion page.
